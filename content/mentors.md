- **[Prof. Liu Jie](https://www.xmu.edu.my/index.php/staff/liu-jie)** (Xiamen University Malaysia) supervised my final-year thesis, *Malliavin Calculus for Variance-Optimal Hedging under Rough Volatility*. I am grateful for his advice, guidance and enormous patience, especially his pivotal advice on the theory of the Martingale Representation Theorem. Before this project, I mostly treated mathematics through grades and GPA rules. He has consistently emphasized rigor, patience and grounded work, and taught me to avoid empty rhetoric and unsupported claims. To me, being rigorous while staying faithful to the problem is part of the romance of becoming a mathematician.
- **[Prof. Li Liu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vqfBC6MAAAAJ&hl=en)** (Great Bay University, ThERA Lab) supervised my research internship on autonomous ultrasound scanning, which led to our paper accepted at IROS 2026. His lab was where I first did real engineering research, and where I came to understand, fairly completely, how a research project is carried through from start to finish. The lab values autonomy in both learning and research; though only recently founded, the lab has already had work recognized with an IROS Best Application Paper Award. If you are drawn to similar directions, take a look at [ThERA Lab](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/iRfFLVilZZjBNonzCE0icQ).
- **[Dr. Goh Sim Kuan](https://www.xmu.edu.my/staff/assistant-prof-dr-goh-sim-kuan)** (Xiamen University Malaysia) welcomed me into his multi-agent reading group, where I learned about AI and multi-agent systems. We also had substantive discussions on part of my final-year thesis. He is a teacher who is genuinely warm and generous in supporting students' growth.
