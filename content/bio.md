Hi, I'm Yi Fan, a final-year Mathematics and Applied Mathematics student at Xiamen University Malaysia (GPA 3.83/4.0, top 5%). I work on **stochastic modeling and uncertainty quantification**, around one question: how do we build known structure into a model so that it stays both learnable and explainable?

Most of what I've done so far started without a ready-made path. Coursework gave me the foundation, with **A grades in Real Analysis, Stochastic Processes and Numerical Analysis**, but when rough volatility caught my interest, stochastic calculus itself was beyond the curriculum, so I **taught myself** from *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II* and Malliavin calculus, and turned it into my final-year thesis, *Malliavin Calculus for Variance-Optimal Hedging under Rough Volatility*, completed in my third year and **graded A**.

That work became *Deep Hedging under Rough Volatility: A Fractional Kernel Embedding Approach*, **accepted at *AIMS Mathematics*** (co-first and corresponding author): we build the model's long-memory structure directly into the network and, under a stated approximation assumption, derive a conditional convergence rate for the hedging error. At Great Bay University's ThERA Lab, I handled data processing and statistical analysis for a paper on autonomous ultrasound scanning, **accepted at IROS 2026**.

I formally graduate in June 2027: my programme runs three years, but the degree is conferred under China's four-year system. Having finished all coursework, I will join [Prof. Bizhao Pang](https://bizhao001.github.io/)'s group at HKUST(GZ) as a research intern from November 2026, working on risk assessment for learning-enabled systems, before continuing to graduate study.
