## Rainy Night at Petaling Street

It was an unplanned rainy night. I wandered through Chinatown (Petaling Street) in Kuala Lumpur with my camera, and the rain had turned the entire street into a giant mirror.

Neon reflections flowed in the puddles — red, green, blue — like the city's pulse beating on the ground. An elderly man pushed his folding cart through the alley, wheels cutting through the water, scattering tiny sparks of light.

I crouched by the roadside, setting the shutter speed to 1/15s to capture that flowing sense of light. The final shot, with its interplay of neon reflections and blurred figures in the distance, became my most vivid impression of this city — where繁华 and street life coexist, where modernity and tradition breathe together.
