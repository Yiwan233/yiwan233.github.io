## Murals and Memories

The street art of Georgetown, Penang is already world-famous, but seeing it in person still hits differently — that feeling of "art seamlessly woven into daily life."

The mural that left the deepest impression wasn't at a popular tourist spot. It was hidden in an unassuming alley: a schoolgirl flying a kite. The kite string extended all the way to a real electric pole, blurring the line between painted and physical reality.

The afternoon sun was fierce that day. I waited for the exact moment when a beam of light fell directly on the mural before pressing the shutter. A passing grandmother paused, looked at me, then looked at the mural, and said something in Hokkien with a smile — I didn't catch the words, but that smile itself was a story.

> Georgetown taught me something: the best street photography isn't about finding spectacular scenes, but discovering extraordinary moments within the ordinary.
