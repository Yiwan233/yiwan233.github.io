## XMUM Before Sunrise

I have a habit — on mornings without early classes, I wake up at 5:30 and walk around campus.

This particular morning was exceptionally quiet. The horizon was just turning pale blue, palm tree silhouettes fading in and out of the morning mist. There was only one senior jogging on the field, and the lights in the distant teaching building had just come on.

I stood on the lawn in front of the library, watching the light gradually shift — from cool blue-grey to warm gold. That transition from dark to light felt like a silent promise: a new day, new possibilities.

This photo needed very little post-processing. Sometimes, reality itself is the best filter.

> Xiamen University Malaysia has a unique charm in the early morning. Tropical vegetation and Chinese architecture blend here, creating an atmosphere I've never experienced anywhere else.
